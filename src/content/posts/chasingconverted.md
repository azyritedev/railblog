On the 15th, I, with a couple of friends chased down V27, a special V set with 
double converted driving trailers running a 4 car Lithgow service. With the V set
retirement and scrapping underway these special carriages are becoming a rarer sight
so I thought we should document them in service before they're all gone.

At the time of filming, V sets have been fully displaced from the CCN line and
only run regular services on the BMT, which helped narrow the search area a lot.

Eventually, after deciding it was too boring to just wait at Sydney Terminal, we
took a regular V set to Emu Plains, and then headed back toward Penrith where we
chanced upon V27 (a double converted) after browsing some fellow transport
enthusiasts' Instagram stories. Big thanks to [Kayden Rayward](https://www.instagram.com/kaydenrayward/) who incidentally posted footage of V27 on the day 
near Parramatta which greatly helped our search!

## Sets with Converted Driving Trailers

*Thanks to [u/Super_Sink_2022](https://reddit.com/u/Super_Sink_2022) and
[u/Discolau](https://reddit.com/u/Discolau) on the
[Sydney Trains subreddit](https://reddit.com/r/sydneytrains) for this information!*

The following sets are **double converts**:

- V21 (DCT9032 & DTD9036)
- V25 (**DET9216** & DCT9039), with DET9216 being a one-of-a-kind former "Contura" car
  featuring longitudinal seating in the vestibule.
- V27 (DCT9031 & DCT9040)

And these have **single converts**:

- V2 (DCT9042)
- V18 (DCT9044)
- V26 (DCT9037)
